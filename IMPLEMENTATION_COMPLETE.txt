KEY DESIGN DECISIONS

✅ WHY TEAL OVER DEFAULT BLUE?
   - Differentiation (overused in SaaS)
   - Industry relevance (construction materials)
   - Premium perceived value
   - Excellent accessibility contrast
   - Sophisticated, not playful

✅ WHY SYSTEM FONTS?
   - Zero web font downloads
   - Perfect rendering everywhere
   - Industry standard (Linear, Stripe)
   - Instant page rendering

✅ WHY CSS CUSTOM PROPERTIES?
   - Single source of truth
   - Easy maintenance
   - Great developer experience
   - Future theme support
